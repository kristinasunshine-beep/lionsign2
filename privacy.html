Privacy

This page explains the data used by the project inquiry form on lionsignstudio.com.

What the form sends

When you submit an inquiry, the form sends the email address you enter and the service you select to MailerLite, which is used to process the form submission.

Why it is used

The information is collected so LIONSIGN can review and respond to your project inquiry. Do not include confidential or sensitive information in the form.

Third-party processing

MailerLite processes the form submission as an external service provider. Its own privacy terms apply to that processing.

Your request

If you want to ask about information submitted through the site, use the project inquiry route and state that your message concerns privacy.
