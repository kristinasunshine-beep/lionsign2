What are LIONSIGN Studio's core activities?
+
LIONSIGN works across identity systems, graphic design and marketing, landscape architecture and limited apparel artwork
Reading the numbered cards
+
Read the numbered cards from left to right as four entry points into the studio. Each number opens a dedicated page with its own context, examples and next step, while the contact form stays on the portal
Where do inquiries start?
+
The portal keeps every request in one place. The dedicated pages help visitors choose the right path before the live form collects the project details
Refining existing material
+
Existing material is treated as raw identity evidence. What already carries recognition is kept, what creates noise is removed, and the result becomes a clearer visual language for web, content, print and client-facing touchpoints
Combining studio directions
+
Yes. The numbered cards are entry points with room to combine; a project can connect identity, digital graphic work, spatial architecture and limited apparel into one coherent direction
What should be prepared before contact?
+
A useful inquiry includes the project goal, audience, current materials, preferred direction and the first challenge that needs clarity
